---
title: "Post-Disposition Defense Guide"
---

Open City Advocates provides support to attorneys on the DC Superior Court's Post-Commitment Juvenile Attorney Panel. Our resources are available to all youth defenders.

Our Post-Disposition Defense Manual follows a case from the commitment order to the end of DYRS care. The 2026 edition is updated for the ROAD Act, DYRS's October 2026 policies and the court's post-commitment practice standards. It covers community and secure placements, Community Status Review Hearings, disciplinary hearings, education, clients with adult charges, and the transition out of DYRS care.

The manual is online for youth defenders, by application.
