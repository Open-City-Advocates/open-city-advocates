Open City Advocates provides support to attorneys on the DC Superior Court's Post-Commitment Juvenile Attorney Panel. Our resources are available to all youth defenders.

## Request Access

Youth defenders can request access to the Post-Disposition Defense Manual by email. Tell us your name and organization, and confirm that you are a youth defender.
