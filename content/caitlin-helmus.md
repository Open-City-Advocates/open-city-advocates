Caitlin is a Staff Attorney at Open City Advocates who is focused on client-centered advocacy for young people in the juvenile legal system. Caitlin comes to Open City Advocates from the Missouri State Public Defender System (MSPD), where she worked as a Trial Attorney in the St. Louis City office. At MSPD, Caitlin worked with individuals charged with misdemeanors and felonies and represented clients from arrest through trial.

Caitlin received her JD from Saint Louis University School of Law and her BS from Indiana University. In law school, Caitlin was a Research and Writing teaching assistant as well as President of the Criminal Law Society and the Public Interest Law Group.
